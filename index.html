Serving Our Community With the Love of Jesus Christ

Every Saturday we gather in Kansas City with one purpose—to share the love of Jesus Christ through compassion, fellowship, prayer, and practical support. Whether it's a warm breakfast, a hot cup of coffee, or simply a listening ear, we believe every act of kindness reflects God's love.

Welcome

United In Grace Ministries is a Christ-centered outreach dedicated to serving our neighbors experiencing homelessness throughout Kansas City with compassion, dignity, and the love of Jesus Christ. Every Saturday, in partnership with Hope Faith Homeless Assistance Campus, our volunteers gather to prepare and serve hot breakfasts, fresh coffee, bottled water, hygiene supplies, clothing, prayer, encouragement, and practical resources to individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.

Our ministry was founded on a simple belief: every person is created in the image of God and deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, kindness, and genuine compassion. We believe that sometimes the greatest act of ministry begins with a warm meal, a friendly conversation, a listening ear, or a heartfelt prayer. Through these simple acts of service, we strive to build meaningful relationships that reflect the unconditional love and grace of Jesus Christ.

While meeting physical needs is an important part of what we do, our mission reaches far beyond providing food or essential supplies. We desire to bring hope to those who may feel forgotten, encourage those facing hardship, pray with anyone seeking comfort, and share the life-changing message of the Gospel. Every conversation is an opportunity to remind someone that they are deeply loved by God, valued as an individual, and never alone.

We are grateful to serve alongside Hope Faith Homeless Assistance Campus, local churches, community organizations, generous donors, and dedicated volunteers who share our passion for making a lasting difference. Together, we are building a stronger community where people are cared for, relationships are strengthened, and lives are touched through faithful service and the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Whether you are looking for a place to volunteer, donate supplies, support our outreach financially, partner with our ministry, or simply pray for those we serve, we invite you to become part of what God is doing through United In Grace Ministries. Every act of generosity, every prayer, every hour volunteered, and every encouraging word helps extend Christ's love to our neighbors and makes a meaningful impact in our community.
