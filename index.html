Sharing the Love of Jesus Christ

Every Saturday, we gather in Kansas City with one purpose—to share the love of Jesus Christ through compassion, fellowship, prayer, and practical support. Whether it's a warm breakfast, a hot cup of coffee, or simply a listening ear, we believe every act of kindness reflects God's love.

Our Mission

Our mission is to demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ by serving people experiencing homelessness through compassion, practical support, meaningful relationships, and the hope of the Gospel.

Saturday Outreach

    ❤️ Compassion Serving every person with dignity and respect. ☕ Outreach Fresh coffee, breakfast, prayer and fellowship every Saturday. 🤝 Community Partnering with churches and organizations. ✝️ Faith Sharing the hope and love found in Jesus Christ.

How You Can Help

Volunteer, donate supplies, partner through your church, or pray for our ministry as we share Christ's love with our Kansas City community.

Get Involved

We welcome volunteers, church partners, and community organizations who share our passion for serving our neighbors with compassion and the love of Jesus Christ. Whether you'd like to volunteer, donate supplies, pray, or partner with us, we'd love to connect with you.
