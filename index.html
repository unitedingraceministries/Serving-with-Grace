Sharing the Love of Jesus Christ

Every Saturday we gather in Kansas City with one purpose—to share the love of Jesus Christ through compassion, fellowship, prayer, and practical support. Whether it's a warm breakfast, a hot cup of coffee, or simply a listening ear, we believe every act of kindness reflects God's love.

Welcome

United In Grace Ministries exists to demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ through compassion, service, prayer, and meaningful relationships.

We believe every individual deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, kindness, and unconditional love. Our ministry is centered on meeting practical needs while sharing the hope of the Gospel.

Our Mission

Our mission is to demonstrate the love of Jesus Christ by serving people experiencing homelessness through compassion, practical support, meaningful relationships, and the hope of the Gospel.

How You Can Help

There are many ways you can become part of what God is doing through United In Grace Ministries. Whether you volunteer your time, donate supplies, partner through your church, or simply pray for our ministry, every act of generosity makes a difference.

❤️ Become a Grace Partner

Every Saturday, God opens doors for United In Grace Ministries to serve our neighbors experiencing homelessness with hot meals, fresh coffee, prayer, encouragement, and the hope of Jesus Christ. None of this is possible without faithful people who choose to pray, give, volunteer, and serve alongside us. When you become a Grace Partner, you become part of every life touched through this ministry.
